Questions

What do I need to install it?

Mac: Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, about 4 GB of disk. Open the download and drag Pianpian to Applications.

Windows: a 64-bit (x64) PC with Windows 11 recommended; Windows 10 isn’t tested yet and Windows on ARM isn’t supported. It needs Microsoft’s WebView2, which Windows 11 includes (not checked on a clean install). The installer is for your user only and needs no admin rights. Plus space for your videos.

What happens on first launch?

Choose where to keep your videos and tools. Setup then downloads, with progress:

Mac: the engine (about 0.65 GB), Whisper (1.6 GB) and Hy-MT2 (1 GB).
Windows: the engine (about 0.46 GB), Whisper for whisper.cpp (1.62 GB), Hy-MT2 for llama.cpp (1.13 GB) and a runtime for your hardware: about 30 MB on the CPU, 50 MB with Vulkan, 1.35 GB with an NVIDIA GPU (CUDA).

It compares download sources and mirrors, including those reachable from mainland China.

Which AI models does Pianpian use?

Whisper transcribes the audio; Hy-MT2 translates the subtitles into English. Both run on your computer (MLX on a Mac; whisper.cpp and llama.cpp on Windows). Your videos stay with you, and no account or API key is needed. The app connects to download videos, models, tools and updates.

Where are my videos and subtitles saved?

In the library folder you choose during setup (default: ~/Movies/Pianpian on a Mac, Videos\Pianpian on Windows). Each video has its own folder with the video and its Chinese, pinyin and English subtitle files.

Demo files: Video · Chinese · Pinyin · English.

Are the subtitles always accurate?

Transcription, translation and grammar hints can be wrong. Check them against the audio. The library’s HSK level estimates vocabulary difficulty, not listening difficulty.

Can the website demo send notes to Anki?

No: export from the local Pianpian app, with Anki Desktop running and AnkiConnect installed. The website demo lets you try the player. Anki export isn’t tested on Windows yet.

Why does my computer warn me about the app?

Pianpian isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet. Mac: after the first try to open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. Windows: if SmartScreen stops the installer, choose More info → Run anyway. You can read the source code first.

How do I uninstall it?

Mac: move Pianpian from Applications to the Bin. Windows: uninstall it from Settings → Apps. Both keep your library and the tools folder (engine and models, several GB; by default ~/Library/Application Support/Pianpian on a Mac, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pianpian on Windows). Delete that folder to free the space.

Is there a phone or Linux app?

No. Pianpian runs on Mac and Windows computers.
