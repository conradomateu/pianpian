Pianpian 片片 · demo

This is the real app. Play the video, hover a word, open the Subtitles and Grammar menus. You can also drop a video of your own on it: it plays from your disk and nothing is uploaded.

Any videoBilibili, YouTube, or your own files

Use the platform's subtitles when there are some, or Whisper hears the Chinese for you. Burned-in subtitles are read with macOS's own OCR.

Three lines中文, pinyin and English

Show any mix. Pinyin is coloured by tone, and it can sit right above each word instead of on its own line.

Every wordHover to see the meaning

Each word shows its pinyin, a dictionary meaning, its HSK level and its measure words, and lights up in the English too.

ListeningFollow the voice

The word being said lights up as it is spoken. Loop a line, pause after each one, or slow it down.

GrammarSee how a sentence is built

A cheat sheet for word order and time words, and optional marks under 了, 过, 在 and 要 that tell you what each one does.

PrivateRuns on your Mac

Speech recognition and translation are local models. No account, no API key, no upload. Videos stay in a folder you choose.

How it works

Paste a link

Or drop a video file. Each video is ready to watch in about a minute per ten minutes of video.

It makes the subtitles

Chinese from the platform or from Whisper, pinyin with jieba and pypinyin, English from Tencent's Hy-MT2 model.

Watch and click

Click any line to jump to it. Everything is also saved as an MKV file with all the tracks, for IINA, VLC or mpv.
